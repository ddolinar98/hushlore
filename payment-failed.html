That didn't go through

Nothing was charged. Your card was declined, or the payment was cancelled before it finished.

What usually fixes it

  • Try a different card. Some banks decline anything they read as adult or foreign, on the first attempt.
  • Check the card details, especially the billing postcode.
  • Prepaid and virtual cards are often refused outright.
  • If your bank sent you a confirmation prompt, it has to be approved within a couple of minutes or the payment lapses.
